Meet Brent Eugene Auringer

With over 30 years of industry experience as a Business Consultant and SAP Solutions Architect, Brent brings an unmatched depth of technical expertise and strategic vision to every project at BEA Designs.

Based in Austin, MN, BEA Designs offers end-to-end project management from initial needs assessment to delivery of precise, custom drawing sets. We specialize in equitable CAD design utilizing the full power of Autodesk technologies and Revit.

  • Revit-proficient CAD design for residential & commercial
  • End-to-end project management & delivery
  • Advanced BIM collaboration with AI integration
  • 30+ years business consulting background
